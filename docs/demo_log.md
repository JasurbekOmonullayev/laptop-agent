**Client:** Under what conditions can a laptop of proper quality be returned?

*Tools: `search_documents({"query": "возврат товара надлежащего качества условия срок"})` · 9.9 s*

**Agent:**

Based on the trade agreement, here's the full picture:

**General rule for returning items of proper quality (clause 6.5.1):**
A buyer may return a non-food item of proper quality within **10 calendar days** of purchase if it didn't suit them in size, color, technical characteristics, or for other reasons.

**Conditions (clause 6.5.2):**
The return is possible only if:
- The item has **not been used**
- The packaging with barcode (QR code) and **ASL BELGISI** digital marking is preserved
- Factory labels, tags, and seals are intact
- The item retains its **commercial appearance** and consumer properties
- The document confirming the purchase is available

**However, laptops are explicitly excluded (clause 6.5.5):**

The clause lists categories of non-food goods of proper quality that are **not eligible for return or exchange**, regulated by the Cabinet of Ministers of Uzbekistan Resolution No. 75 dated 13.02.2003. Among them:

> "Технически сложные товары бытового назначения, на которые установлены гарантийные сроки (… **бытовая вычислительная и множительная техника** …)."

A laptop is "бытовая вычислительная техника" (household computing equipment), so it falls on this exclusion list.

**In short:** Even though the general 10-day return rule exists, a laptop of proper quality **cannot be returned or exchanged** because it is specifically listed as an item not subject to return.
